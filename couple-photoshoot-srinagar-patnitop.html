Srinagar

Couple Photoshoot in Srinagar

Looking for a couple photographer in Srinagar? We can arrange romantic couple shoots and cinematic reels in Srinagar around Dal Lake, houseboats, the Mughal Gardens and mountain viewpoints. Permissions, entry rules and conditions vary by place and season, so we confirm what is possible before your shoot.

Dal Lake and houseboats

Shikaras, houseboats and mountain reflections make Dal Lake one of Kashmir's best-known backdrops. We confirm which lakeside spots and houseboats can be used for a shoot.

Mughal Gardens

Historic terraced gardens beside Dal Lake, such as Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh, known for chinar trees, flowers and fountains in season. Entry rules and photography permissions vary, so we check first.

Gardens and mountain viewpoints

Other gardens and hillside viewpoints around the city, including the tulip garden when it is open in spring. What is possible depends on the time of year.

Patnitop

Couple Photoshoot and Pre-Wedding Shoot in Patnitop

A hill station in Jammu & Kashmir, Patnitop offers pine forests, open meadows and wide mountain viewpoints for a couple photoshoot or a pre-wedding shoot in Patnitop. Our team is based locally in Patnitop.

Pine forests

Tall pine-lined paths and quiet forest light, away from the main road.

Meadows

Open grassland at the heart of Patnitop, with wide mountain views.

Nathatop and Sanasar viewpoints

Nathatop is a panoramic viewpoint above Patnitop, and Sanasar is a bowl-shaped meadow valley a short drive away.

Couple Photoshoot FAQs

Do you offer a couple photographer in Srinagar?
Patnitop Travels Hub can arrange a professional photographer for couple photoshoots in Srinagar, around places such as Dal Lake, houseboats, Mughal Gardens and mountain viewpoints. Send your date and shoot type on WhatsApp at 9103331334 and we will confirm availability.
Do you arrange a couple photoshoot in Patnitop?
Yes, we can arrange couple photoshoots in Patnitop's pine forests, meadows and viewpoints such as Nathatop and Sanasar. Share your date and preferred spot and we will confirm what can be arranged.
Can you arrange a pre-wedding photographer in Kashmir?
Yes. Pre-wedding and engagement shoots can be arranged in Srinagar and in Patnitop. Tell us your date, location and the look you want, and we will confirm what is possible.
Do you make cinematic reels in Srinagar?
You can ask for cinematic Instagram reels and couple travel videos in Srinagar and Patnitop, as photos, reels or both. Tell us what you want and we will include it in your quotation.
