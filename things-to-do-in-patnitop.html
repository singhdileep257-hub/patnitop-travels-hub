Things to Do in Patnitop

Places, activities and day-trip ideas around Patnitop, with taxi help from Patnitop Travels Hub.

Patnitop is a hill station in Jammu & Kashmir, popular for cool weather, meadows and mountain views. Here are common ideas for a visit. Patnitop Travels Hub is a local taxi and travel-assistance service: we can take you there, arrange your pickup, and plan a route that fits your time.

Viewpoints and meadow walks

Nathatop, Sanasar and the open meadows of Patnitop are the usual starting points for easy walks and mountain views.

The forested hills around Patnitop are popular for short walks and day treks. Ask us to drop you at a starting point and arrange the pickup time.

Snow in winter

Patnitop sometimes receives snowfall in winter, but snow is not guaranteed and varies from year to year. Road and weather conditions can change quickly, so please check with us before you travel.

Temples and pilgrimage stops

Naag Temple near Patnitop, and Sudh Mahadev, Mantalai and Gauri Kund in the hills nearby, are often combined into one day trip.

What are the best things to do in Patnitop?
Popular choices include the Nathatop viewpoint, Sanasar meadow, walks around Patnitop Meadows, nearby temples such as Naag Temple and Sudh Mahadev, and, depending on season and availability, activities such as the gondola ride, zip line, trekking, horse riding, shikara boating and snow visits.
