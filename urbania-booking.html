Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an Urbania?
Call 9103331334 or send the enquiry form with your pickup point, date, destination and number of people. We confirm availability and the rate before booking.
How many people does an Urbania seat?
Seating can differ, so we confirm the exact seating for your trip when you enquire. Tell us your group size.
Should I choose an Urbania or a Tempo Traveller?
It depends on your group size, luggage and route. Tell us your plan and we will suggest what suits you. See our Tempo Traveller booking page for comparison.
How much does this taxi cost?
We do not publish fixed fares, because the rate depends on your pickup point, date, vehicle and group size. Call 9103331334 (or WhatsApp) and we will tell you the current rate before you book.
Is my taxi confirmed when I send the enquiry form?
No. The form sends us an enquiry. Your taxi is confirmed only when we confirm the vehicle, driver and rate with you directly by call or WhatsApp.
Are the vehicles owned by Patnitop Travels Hub?
Trips are arranged using our vehicles and partner drivers, depending on availability. We confirm the vehicle and driver when you book.
